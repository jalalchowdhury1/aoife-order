# 🎒 Which First? — Aoife's order-of-operations app

Live at **https://aoife-order.vercel.app**

Teaches Aoife which bit of a sum to do first — brackets, then ×, then + — without
ever asking her to memorise BODMAS.

## The idea

Instead of a rule, she gets a reason she can check herself:

> **× builds a bag. + tips everything into a pile.
> You can't tip out a bag you haven't filled yet.**

So `3 × 4` isn't "a three and a four" — it's **one number** (twelve) that hasn't been
written out yet. Brackets are glue she can draw herself: whatever's inside becomes a
bag, which is why brackets go before the ×.

She starts with real sweets and no symbols at all, and only meets `2 + 3 × 4` once
she's already worked out why the bags have to be filled first.

## How it works

14 levels in 4 groups. Every level has the same five buttons as her other apps —
**👀 Watch → 🤝 Together → 🧭 Lead → 🦋 Solo → 🔁 Practice** — so she sees it done,
does it with help, chooses the moves herself, then does it alone.

1. **🍬 Bags and Piles** — count real sweets, see it written down, then watch someone
   count left to right and get sweets that aren't there.
2. **🔨 Spot the Builder** — find the ×, wherever it's hiding. Including sums with no
   × at all, so she learns to check rather than react.
3. **🫧 The Bubble** — brackets. The big one is level 9: the same three numbers with
   and without brackets, both worked out, landing on different answers.
4. **🕵️ Detective** — mark someone else's work, place the brackets yourself, then a
   final level that mixes every shape at random.

There's one line on screen the whole time. The bit she's about to do lights up, then
turns into its answer where it stood, and the line gets shorter — finishing as the
whole statement, `(2 × 2) + 8 = 12`.

No timer, no accounts, nothing to log into. Progress is saved on the iPad itself.
Tap the title five times quickly for a parent's progress view.

Subtraction and division are coming later — the app is built so they slot into the
same two ideas without her having to learn anything new.
